# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, whether in issues, pull requests, reviews or discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent
- keep feedback about the work, not the person
- accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what gets merged

These aren't acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- personal attacks or trolling
- publishing someone else's private information
- spam, including bulk or automated low-effort issues and pull requests

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block anyone who breaks these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
